Mobile 2019–21

Digitizing parcel delivery across New Caledonia.

Mobile mail and parcel delivery app for OPT: parcel scanning and on-site customer signature capture.

OPT Nouméa · via Skazy NC

01 The need

New Caledonia's post and telecom office (OPT) wanted to streamline parcel delivery: scanning parcels and capturing proof of delivery in the field, replacing paper processes.

02 The solution

Working within the postal IS office through Skazy NC, I built the SIDO MOBILE app used by delivery agents: parcel scanning, route tracking and customer signature capture on delivery.

  • Parcel scanning and route tracking on the move
  • Customer signature captured directly on mobile
  • Ionic/Angular app backed by Spring Boot

03 Technologies

  • Ionic
  • Angular
  • Spring Boot

The results

End-to-end traceability for OPT agents — and no more paper slips at delivery.
